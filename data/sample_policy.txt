Privacy Policy — Super Calculator v2.3

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Device Information
We collect your device's Android ID (a random, resettable identifier) to distinguish
your device for analytics purposes. This identifier cannot be used to identify you
personally and is reset when you factory-reset your device.

We do NOT collect your IMEI, IMSI, phone number, or any other persistent hardware
identifier.

1.2 Usage Analytics
We collect anonymised usage statistics, including which calculator features you use
most frequently, to improve the application. All analytics data is anonymised before
transmission and does not include any personally identifiable information.

1.3 Location Data
We do NOT collect, access, or process location data of any kind.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

2.1 We use anonymised device identifiers solely to:
   - Count unique installations for internal analytics.
   - Detect and prevent abuse of the free tier.

2.2 We do not sell, rent, or share any collected data with third parties.

3. DATA RETENTION
Analytics data is retained for no longer than 90 days and is then permanently deleted.

4. YOUR RIGHTS
You may opt out of analytics collection at any time in Settings > Privacy.
